# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, potential clients, and fellow developers evaluating
Jon Wayne Cabusbusan (jnwync) as a remote full-stack web and mobile hire or
freelance partner. They skim first (10–30s), then verify: what shipped, what he
owned, which stack, how to reach him. Viewed on office laptops and phones;
recruiter usability outranks spectacle.

### Brand Personality
Shipped, deliberate, quietly confident. "jnwync." is the brand: lowercase
Bricolage Grotesque wordmark whose period is the single accent that ever takes
the brand green — the "active" signal. Technical without terminal cosplay;
personable without gimmicks.

### Aesthetic Direction
Quiet depth in two weathers. One green family (deep pine ink → racing green →
soft leaf) on a dimensional field that moves at 10–20% of scroll while the
content stays near-static. Dark (default): deep green-black void, nebula lift,
sparse three-depth starfield. Light: warm paper, horizon band, soft layered
shapes, bokeh motes — atmosphere and depth of field, never an inverted dark
mode. Only two opaque surfaces on the page (the Work and Contact plates); every
other section lets the field through. Type stays flat and sharp in both —
the contrast between crisp type and the soft field is the whole effect.
Signature moment: the stack row under the hero, Simple Icons settling flat from
loose 3D space as they scroll into view. Everything else stays calm so it lands.
Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display + wordmark), Hanken Grotesk (body),
Fragment Mono (kickers, metrics, labels — sparingly). Anti-references: neon
glow, glassmorphism, floating card grids, tilt-on-hover cards, purple nebulae,
AI-slop hero templates, anything that reads as a screensaver.

### Design Principles
1. Identity over decoration — every accent traces to the wordmark's green dot;
   the field is procedural (canvas, CSS), never a downloaded texture.
2. One depth model — fixed field, transparent sections, lifted plates. Depth is
   read from scale, parallax rate and surface, not from effects on type.
3. My work over UI chrome — real projects, real metrics from data/portfolio.ts,
   never invented.
4. Motion is scroll-position, never time: one easing family (ease-out-quint),
   transforms and opacity only, full prefers-reduced-motion collapse, nothing
   blocking longer than a second, no scroll hijacking.
5. Recruiter-first hierarchy — View work / Resume / Contact always one glance
   away; experience rows, dates and contact links stay conventional; AA
   contrast in both weathers; socials as icon buttons (44px targets).
