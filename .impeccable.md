# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, potential clients, and fellow developers evaluating
Jon Wayne Cabusbusan (jnwync) as a remote full-stack web and mobile hire or
freelance partner. They skim first (10–30s), then verify: what shipped, what he
owned, which stack, how to reach him. Viewed on office laptops and phones;
recruiter usability outranks spectacle.

### Brand Personality
Shipped, deliberate, quietly confident. "jnwync." is the brand: lowercase
Bricolage Grotesque wordmark whose period is the single accent that ever takes
the brand green — the "active" signal. Technical without terminal cosplay;
personable without gimmicks.

### Aesthetic Direction
One place, two weathers, and the day passes as you scroll. The whole page sits
in a single continuous scene: a horizon over water. Dark (default) is night —
stars, a moonlit sea; light is day — a warm sky, sunlit water. The theme toggle
changes the time of day of the same place, never swaps a palette. Scrolling is
the passage of hours: the sun or moon starts as the period of the hero masthead,
arcs right, and has set by the contact bookend, whose own period stays lit as a
beacon on the water. The page opens on the water and closes on the water; the
Work plate is the one opaque surface that interrupts it, and it casts a shadow
onto the water above its edge. Type stays flat and sharp against all of it — the
contrast between crisp type and the soft world is the whole effect. Signature
moments, in order: the intro (a horizon draws, the green dot rises into the
wordmark's period), the stack surfacing through the water line as fourteen
points of light, the pinned work stage where one framed window holds still while
three projects pass through it, and the experience axis showing where
engagements overlapped. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display + wordmark, with the
width axis scrubbed on scroll), Hanken Grotesk (body), Fragment Mono (kickers,
metrics, labels — sparingly). Anti-references: neon glow, glassmorphism,
floating card grids, tilt-on-hover cards, purple nebulae, identical fade-ups,
decorative counters, custom cursors, AI-slop hero templates, anything that reads
as a screensaver.

### Design Principles
1. Identity over decoration — every accent traces to the wordmark's green dot;
   the field is procedural (canvas, CSS), never a downloaded texture.
2. One depth model — fixed field, transparent sections, lifted plates. Depth is
   read from scale, parallax rate and surface, not from effects on type.
3. My work over UI chrome — real projects, real metrics from data/portfolio.ts,
   never invented.
4. Motion is scroll-position, never time: one easing family (ease-out-quint),
   transforms and opacity only, one animation loop, pinning by CSS sticky so
   native scrolling and assistive tech are untouched, nothing blocking longer
   than a second, no scroll hijacking. Every animation must direct attention,
   reveal information, reinforce hierarchy, create continuity or establish
   personality — decoration ships nothing. See docs/design-system/motion.md.
5. Readability is enforced, not eyeballed: the night scene is luminance-capped
   and tests/world-contrast.test.mjs proves body text clears AA against the
   brightest background the moving world can produce.
6. Recruiter-first hierarchy — View work / Resume / Contact always one glance
   away; experience rows, dates and contact links stay conventional; AA
   contrast in both weathers; socials as icon buttons (44px targets).
