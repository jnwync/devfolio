# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, potential clients, and fellow developers evaluating
Jon Wayne Cabusbusan (jnwync) as a full-stack hire or freelance partner. They skim
first (10–30s), then verify: what shipped, what he owned, which stack, how to reach
him. Viewed on office laptops and phones; recruiter usability outranks spectacle.

### Brand Personality
Shipped, deliberate, quietly confident. "jnwync." is the brand: lowercase
Bricolage Grotesque wordmark whose period is the single accent that ever takes
the brand green — the "active" signal. Technical without terminal cosplay;
personable without gimmicks.

### Aesthetic Direction
Racing-green + warm paper editorial system. One green family (deep pine ink →
racing green → soft leaf on dark), green-tinted neutrals, NO purple, NO orange
accent, NO Matrix/neon/terminal green. Mixed surfaces: warm paper base with two
dark green-ink "scenes" (Selected Work, Contact). Type: Bricolage Grotesque
(display + wordmark), Hanken Grotesk (body), Fragment Mono (kickers, metrics,
periods — sparingly). Anti-references: generic dark dev portfolio, glowing
gradient cards, floating tech logos, AI-slop hero templates.

### Design Principles
1. Identity over decoration — every accent traces to the wordmark's green dot.
2. Composition over effects — scene changes (cover, horizontal drive, lift
   reveal) carry the memorability; content between them stays calm.
3. My work over UI chrome — real projects, real metrics from data/portfolio.ts,
   never invented.
4. One easing family (cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1) / ease-out-quint), transforms
   and opacity only, full prefers-reduced-motion collapse.
5. Recruiter-first hierarchy — View work / Resume / Contact always one glance
   away; socials as icon buttons (44px targets).
